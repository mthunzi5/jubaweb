Placement

Learner Sourcing and Placement

We source suitable learners, manage the placement process, and support work-integrated learning so programmes lead to real workplace opportunities.

Our value-added learner placement services help employers identify suitable candidates for learnerships, internships, and skills programmes while supporting broader workforce development and B-BBEE objectives. We manage key administration on behalf of our clients, including screening, validation, learner readiness checks, and coordination of registration requirements with the relevant SETA structures.

We invest in community outreach and recruitment efforts to source learners who are committed, future-focused, and ready to complete their programmes successfully. Our approach prioritizes learners who are serious about career development and employers who want reliable, supported candidates entering their training pipeline.

Our Placement Support Includes

  • Learner sourcing aligned to programme and employer requirements
  • Screening, validation, and onboarding administration
  • SETA-related learner coordination and registration support
  • Workplace placement planning and employer matching

How We Source Learners

We work through community outreach, recruitment drives, and structured learner engagement channels to identify candidates who are suitable for funded and employer-sponsored opportunities.

How We Support Employers

We reduce the administrative burden by coordinating the learner intake process, preparing documentation, and helping clients place candidates into programmes with confidence.

How We Support Learners

We aim to place learners in environments where they can gain practical work exposure, complete programme requirements, and build a path toward sustainable employment.

Placement Partners

Work-integrated learning is essential to the success of many learnerships. For this reason, we work with placement partners who can provide supervised practical experience to enrolled learners. This creates value for both the learner and the host business by building skills while extending workplace capacity.

Where possible, we also support employer absorption and progression opportunities at the end of the programme. Our objective is not only programme completion, but meaningful workplace transition and stronger employment outcomes.

Who Can Become a Placement Partner?

  • A registered business with sound workplace ethics and suitable supervision capacity
  • A workplace that is accessible to learners and reasonably reachable through public transport
  • A safe environment, including accessibility for disabled learners where applicable
  • An appointed mentor who can guide the learner and support work logbook completion
  • A workstation and practical exposure aligned to the learner's field of study
  • Readiness to participate in site verification and placement quality processes

Strategic Planning & Compliance

Workplace Skills Plan (WSP) Compilation & Submission

We compile comprehensive annual Workplace Skills Plans for SETA submission based on your company's strategic objectives, workplace needs, and employee personal records. Our WSPs are designed to address critical scarce skills needs in your industry while ensuring alignment with Employment Equity (EE) and Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment (BBBEE) requirements.

  • Strategic alignment with business objectives
  • EE and BBBEE integration
  • Industry-specific skills gap analysis
  • SETA compliance guarantee

Annual Training Report (ATR) Completion & Submission

Accurate and timely ATR submissions to demonstrate training implementation and unlock mandatory grant benefits. We ensure your organization meets all SETA reporting requirements while maximizing grant recovery opportunities.
